# Example meals by type

## Breakfast examples

**Savory Frittata Breakfast Plate**
Egg frittata with sweet apple chicken sausage links on the side — like a diner breakfast plate.
- *Protein* — Egg Frittata (2 frittatas)
- *Side protein/fat* — Sweet Apple Chicken Sausage (2 links)
- *~510 cal | 25g P / 18g C / 30g F*

**Smoked Salmon & Yogurt Breakfast Bowl**
Smoked salmon over a thick Greek yogurt base with a squeeze of lemon — a no-cook, high-protein start.
- *Protein* — Norwegian Smoked Salmon (full package, 4 oz)
- *Sauce / dairy base* — Strained Thick & Creamy Greek Yogurt Plain (3/4 cup)
- *~390 cal | 43g P / 12g C / 18g F*

**Protein Pancake Stack with Skyr**
Fluffy protein pancakes stacked and topped with Icelandic-style skyr in place of syrup — a sweet, high-protein morning stack.
- *Carb + protein* — Protein Pancakes (2 packages)
- *Sauce / dairy layer* — Icelandic Style Skyr Lowfat Vanilla Yogurt (1 container)
- *~570 cal | 50g P / 60g C / 10g F*

**Harvest Egg White Bites + Pork Sausage Plate**
Protein-rich egg white bites paired with crumbled pork sausage — a quick, diner-style plate.
- *Protein* — Harvest Vegetable Hash Egg White Bites (1 package)
- *Side protein/fat* — Country Style Ground Pork Sausage (1 serving, crumbled and pan-fried)
- *~280 cal | 25g P / 14g C / 14g F* (supplement with a piece of fruit or toast to hit calorie target)

## Lunch examples

**Chicken Caesar Wrap**
The grab-and-go TJ's Caesar wrap — oven-roasted chicken, romaine, Parmesan, Caesar dressing in a flour tortilla. Eaten as-is, it's a full lunch.
- *All-in-one* — Chicken Caesar Wrap (1 container)
- *~660 cal | 33g P / 55g C / 32g F*

**Spicy Peanut Noodle Bowl**
Cold-ish noodle bowl with shredded chicken and a peanut-chili sauce — lunch that eats like takeout.
- *All-in-one* — Spicy Peanutty Noodle Bowl with Chicken (1 container)
- *~500 cal | 19g P / 65g C / 16g F* (pair with a Greek yogurt to boost protein to 35g+)

**Tuna & Red Lentil Pasta Salad**
Yellowfin tuna tossed with cooked red lentil pasta and a drizzle of olive oil — a high-protein cold pasta salad.
- *Protein* — Solid Light Yellowfin Tuna in Olive Oil (1 can, drained)
- *Carb vehicle* — Organic Red Lentil Sedanini (1 serving cooked)
- *~470 cal | 41g P / 55g C / 12g F*

**Chicken & Cheddar Sandwich Plate**
TJ's ready-made chicken sandwich with arugula and sharp white cheddar — no prep needed, full lunch.
- *All-in-one* — Chicken & Cheddar Cheese Sandwich (1 sandwich)
- *~550 cal | 34g P / 48g C / 20g F*

## Dinner examples

**Beef Meatballs over Chickpea Fusilli**
Classic Italian-American: Angus beef meatballs simmered in marinara over chickpea pasta. High protein, high fiber.
- *Protein* — Homestyle Angus Beef Meatballs (4 meatballs / 2 servings)
- *Carb vehicle* — Chickpea Fusilli Pasta (1.5 servings cooked)
- *~760 cal | 45g P / 80g C / 28g F*

**Shawarma Chicken Thighs with Tzatziki over Rice**
Middle Eastern weeknight plate: spiced chicken thighs roasted and served over rice with yogurt sauce.
- *Protein* — Shawarma Chicken Thighs (2 servings / 8 oz)
- *Carb vehicle* — (rice, cooked from pantry staple)
- *Sauce layer* — Strained Thick & Creamy Greek Yogurt Plain (3/4 cup)
- *~550 cal | 52g P / 45g C / 16g F*

**Blackened Salmon over Red Lentil Pasta**
Seared spiced salmon fillet over red lentil sedanini with a yogurt-dill drizzle — a restaurant-quality weeknight dinner.
- *Protein* — Blackened Salmon Seasoned Boneless Fillet (4 oz)
- *Carb vehicle* — Organic Red Lentil Sedanini (1 serving dry, cooked al dente)
- *Sauce layer* — Strained Thick & Creamy Greek Yogurt Plain (1/4 cup)
- *~520 cal | 43g P / 55g C / 14g F*

**Korean Beef Short Ribs with Rice**
Galbi-style short ribs served over steamed white rice — a fast, intensely flavored dinner.
- *Protein* — Korean Style Beef Short Ribs (2 pieces)
- *Carb vehicle* — (rice, cooked from pantry staple)
- *~640 cal | 32g P / 60g C / 26g F*

**Hot Italian Sausage & Chickpea Fusilli**
Sliced Italian pork sausage tossed with chickpea pasta and a simple olive oil and garlic pan sauce — a fast 20-minute pasta dinner.
- *Protein* — Hot Italian Sausage made with Pork (2 links, sliced)
- *Carb vehicle* — Chickpea Fusilli Pasta (2 servings cooked)
- *~760 cal | 52g P / 75g C / 28g F*

**Caramelized Onion Turkey Burger Plate**
Juicy turkey patties with caramelized onion and bell pepper on a bun with a side salad — an American classic done lighter.
- *Protein* — Caramelized Onion & Bell Pepper Turkey Patties (2 patties)
- *Carb vehicle* — (burger buns, pantry staple)
- *Sauce layer* — Strained Thick & Creamy Greek Yogurt Plain
- *~530 cal | 59g P / 42g C / 18g F*
